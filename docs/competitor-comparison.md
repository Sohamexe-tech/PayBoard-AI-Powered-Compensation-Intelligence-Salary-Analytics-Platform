# Competitor comparison

This comparison is based on publicly recognizable product patterns and is used to explain CompIQ's scope. It is not a claim about private implementation details of any competitor.

| Product | Patterns that informed CompIQ | Intentionally not built in this MVP |
|---|---|---|
| Levels.fyi | Company/role/level compensation comparison, career-level framing | Full-scale crowd network, extensive historical market coverage, premium workflows |
| 6figr | Compensation profiles and structured salary exploration | Broad regional product coverage and a large public submission ecosystem |
| AmbitionBox | Company pages, salary discovery, location/role browsing | Employer-review ecosystem, broad employment content, extensive India-specific product surface |
| Glassdoor | Company research, salary visibility, user-submitted information | Reviews, jobs marketplace, employer branding, company community features |

## Inspired features

- Searchable compensation records
- Company pages
- Salary filtering
- Comparison views
- User-submitted compensation data
- Company and role normalization

## Intentionally not built

- Reviews and ratings
- Job listings or recruiting workflows
- Ads or monetization
- Social feeds
- AI recommendations
- Authentication providers and enterprise SSO
- Full moderation/admin tooling
- Universal level equivalence claims

## CompIQ differentiator

CompIQ makes the comparison group explicit:

```text
company-specific level → normalized career level → comparison
```

It keeps the original level visible, calculates total compensation consistently, and displays sample sizes and data-quality boundaries.
